## 结论 · Answer
1. No silent success.
2. 每一次 AI 发起的写都要可验证。Every AI-initiated write must be verifiable.
3. Ownership 不是任务。Ownership is not a task.
4. 先量假成功率，再谈 AI 提效。Measure the false-success rate before claiming AI productivity.
5. 速度和信任不互换。Speed and trust are not traded against each other.
